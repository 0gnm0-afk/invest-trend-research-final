# Literature carried into the 15-year reassessment

This is an extension of the already reviewed research, not a new collection of citations. The prior [method and indicator source review](../uptrend-extension-2026-10-29/literature.md) records the original-text or authoritative formula sources checked before the first extension calculation. The rules, parameters and outcome definitions are retained. The expanded time window changes what can be assessed, but it does not create new independent validation data.

| Verified source carried forward | Decision it changes in this 15-year study | What it does not establish |
|---|---|---|
| Kim, Koh, Boyd & Gorinevsky (2009), [L1 Trend Filtering, author PDF](https://stanford.edu/~boyd/papers/pdf/l1_trend_filter.pdf) | Reuse the exact objective on log close, lambda 1, and existing slope thresholds. Preserve all preceding observations when constructing indicators. Treat the fitted trend as a retrospective evaluation label. Check completed-run and source-endpoint limitations separately from causal signal calculations. | The two-sided fitted trend is not a real-time detector. A longer sample does not make its historically dated starts observable on those dates. The existing endpoint audit is evidence for its audited stocks and dates; it is not proof that every additional historical endpoint is stable. |
| Lo, Mamaysky & Wang (2000), [Foundations of Technical Analysis, author PDF](https://www.mit.edu/~wangj/pap/LoMamayskyWang00.pdf) | Ask whether an explicitly defined pattern carries conditional information. Report early-window precision and whole-uptrend precision against their own base rates. Estimate future paths on all signals and compare with same-stock, same-quarter non-signal observations in observable technical states. | Detecting a conditional distributional difference does not establish a useful entry rule, executable maximum excursion, or profit after costs. |
| Sullivan, Timmermann & White (1999), [LSE FMG working version](https://www.fmg.ac.uk/sites/default/files/2020-11/dp303.pdf) | Freeze the union of previously studied rules rather than selecting new thresholds on the expanded history. Show all candidates and family-adjusted contrasts. Reuse common dependent resampling weights across conditions so comparisons retain shared market-period dependence. | A correction for this fixed family cannot erase decisions made during earlier research, omitted alternatives, or prior inspection of 2016–2025. The earlier date range is a retrospective expansion, not a fresh forward test. |
| Bajgrowicz & Scaillet (2012), [author PDF](https://scaillet.ch/pdfs/BajSca.pdf) | Examine persistence in fixed five-year eras and annual tables; distinguish statistical discovery, persistence and economic usefulness. Keep same-day gains and next-open prices visible. Preserve both adverse excursions and terminal returns instead of presenting only future highs. | Long historical performance, high precision or a positive gross terminal-return difference does not itself establish a tradable strategy, transaction-cost robustness, or stable future performance. |

The indicator implementations remain those documented in the earlier source review: Supertrend, PSAR, TRIX, Stochastic RSI, RMI, Chaikin, calendar-quarter daily AVWAP proxy and volume oscillator. Platform documentation defines a reproducible formula; it is not evidence that the formula confirms an early uptrend. No momentum paper is used as proof of a six-session confirmation claim. Long-horizon momentum and this study's short-horizon event-identification question are different hypotheses.

## Consequences of widening the history

1. **The common primary window is fixed before new results:** 2010-10-01 through 2025-09-30, exactly 180 calendar months. The source continues beyond that window to allow the inherited 126-bar label guard and 63-bar price-path horizon. Older available observations feed the indicators' warmup and the full-history descriptive supplement. Shorter stock histories enter when their observations become evaluable; no artificial 15-year minimum removes newer stocks.
2. **Unequal histories remain visible.** Aggregate signal-weighted precision gives more influence to long and frequently signaling histories. Equal-stock summaries, annual and five-year comparisons, and coverage counts show different parts of that weighting. Neither weighting repairs missing historical firms or unobserved delistings.
3. **“All stored stocks” is a storage-universe statement.** It is not the same as a point-in-time census of every listed stock in each market for every historical year. Research-only inclusion of available histories formerly excluded from operations broadens coverage without changing operational exclusions. Missing, unusable and too-short histories retain explicit reasons.
4. **Longer history does not make observations independent.** Same-stock clustering, shared market months, recurring states and overlapping 5/21/63-bar outcomes remain. The inherited crossed stock and common stationary-month bootstrap uses 3- and 6-month blocks. Its fixed-cell control influence is an approximation; controls are not fully rematched in every replicate. These intervals do not account for every unobserved regime change or selection decision.
5. **Observable-state matching is descriptive adjustment.** The controls use only information available on each observation date, with no retrospective L1 condition in the main path population. Residual standardized differences, common-support loss and control effective sample sizes constrain interpretation. A favorable difference in a narrow supported subset cannot be generalized to every signal.

No parameter, early-window boundary, L1 definition or candidate is changed to improve the expanded results. Any later exploratory analysis must be identified as such. This reassessment can answer whether the existing tradeoffs persist across the available longer history; it cannot by itself demonstrate prospective investment profitability.
